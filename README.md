### 🧑‍💻 About Me

I've been programming for about four years. I started with web development, then spent the next two years diving deeper into backend — working with **Python**, **SQL**, **Git**, a bit of **Django**, and **Selenium**. Along the way I picked up experience deploying projects to **Linux** servers, and even dabbled in **C++**, **C#**, and **Kotlin** (built a small Android app in Android Studio).

These days I'm focused on becoming a well-rounded **FullStack developer** — I've got a solid handle on **JavaScript**, **PHP**, **Sass**, and **Vue.js**, and I'm actively learning **React**. Beyond web dev, I'm exploring **Go** and getting into game development with **Godot Engine** (previously worked with Unreal Engine and Unity — Unreal was more my thing).

My daily drivers are JetBrains IDEs (**PyCharm**, **WebStorm**, **IntelliJ**) alongside **VS Code**.

- 🌍 Based in **Crimea, Simferopol**
- 🧠 Currently learning: **Rust**, **Go**, **Godot Engine**
- 🤝 Open to collaborating on: website development, Telegram/Discord bots, parsers, Python applications, and Go-based projects
